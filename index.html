04

About

I care about one question:

How do we make AI systems scientifically useful, not just benchmark-good?

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Xidian University and a research intern at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University.

My current interests include AI for Drug Discovery, patent-scale scientific information extraction, high-quality ADMET data construction, and representation learning for molecules and proteins.

I am especially interested in systems that connect robust data infrastructure with predictive models that remain useful outside curated benchmarks.

01Evidence first

Predictions should remain traceable to scientific evidence.

02Context matters

Model assay and experimental context, not only molecules.

03Quality is research

Data quality is part of the scientific method, not cleanup.
